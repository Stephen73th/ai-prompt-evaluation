# COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS OF RESULTS

## 1. Introduction

This section examines the results of the 20 experiments conducted using ChatGPT and Google Gemini. The analysis compares the performance of both AI platforms under basic and improved prompting conditions across five task categories: knowledge and explanation, summarisation, creative writing, reasoning and problem solving, and complex instruction following.

Each response was assessed using five criteria, with a maximum possible score of 25 per experiment.

## 2. Comparison of Basic and Improved Prompts

The combined results show that improved prompts achieved 248 out of 250, representing 99.2%, while basic prompts achieved 245 out of 250, representing 98.0%.

| Prompt Type | Total Score | Possible Score | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic | 245 | 250 | 98.0% |
| Improved | 248 | 250 | 99.2% |

The improved prompts achieved a three-point increase over the basic prompts. This suggests that providing clearer instructions, defining expectations and specifying output requirements can improve AI response quality.

However, the difference was relatively small. This indicates that basic prompts were already sufficient to generate strong responses in several tasks.

## 3. Comparison of ChatGPT and Gemini

ChatGPT achieved an overall score of 248 out of 250, equivalent to 99.2%. Gemini achieved 245 out of 250, equivalent to 98.0%.

| AI Platform | Total Score | Possible Score | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ChatGPT | 248 | 250 | 99.2% |
| Gemini | 245 | 250 | 98.0% |

ChatGPT achieved a three-point advantage over Gemini in the combined evaluation. Nevertheless, both platforms demonstrated strong performance across the five tasks.

The results do not establish that ChatGPT is generally superior to Gemini. The difference is small, and the evaluation covers only five task categories and a limited number of responses.

## 4. Analysis by Task Category

### 4.1 Knowledge and Explanation

ChatGPT achieved 25 out of 25 under both prompting conditions. Gemini increased from 23 under the basic prompt to 25 under the improved prompt.

This suggests that additional instructions concerning audience, language, examples and structure helped Gemini produce a more complete and accessible explanation.

### 4.2 Summarisation

Both ChatGPT responses achieved 25 out of 25. Gemini achieved 24 out of 25 under both conditions.

The improved prompt did not increase the total scores in this task. However, it provided more specific requirements concerning length, audience, coverage and writing style. Gemini's improved response was slightly below the required minimum word count, which affected its instruction-following score.

### 4.3 Creative Writing

ChatGPT increased from 23 under the basic prompt to 25 under the improved prompt. Gemini decreased from 25 to 24.

The improved prompt encouraged stronger character development, more deliberate storytelling and clearer plot twists. However, Gemini's improved response contained 391 words against the required minimum of 400, resulting in a deduction for instruction following.

This demonstrates that detailed creative instructions can guide storytelling but do not automatically guarantee compliance with every requirement.

### 4.4 Reasoning and Problem Solving

Both platforms achieved 25 out of 25 under basic and improved prompting conditions.

The basic prompt was already sufficiently clear to produce the correct calculation. Improved instructions encouraged more detailed calculations and independent verification, but they did not increase the scores.

### 4.5 Complex Instruction Following

Both platforms achieved full marks under both prompting conditions.

The basic prompts generated useful workshop schedules, while the improved prompts provided greater control over timing, formatting, session durations and the number of learning outcomes and materials.

The results suggest that improved prompts can increase precision even when the total evaluation score remains unchanged.

## 5. Key Comparative Findings

The experiment produced five important findings:

1. Both AI platforms performed strongly across all five task categories.
2. Improved prompts achieved a slightly higher combined score than basic prompts.
3. ChatGPT recorded a marginally higher overall score than Gemini in this experiment.
4. The greatest positive change occurred in Gemini's knowledge and explanation task, where its score increased from 23 to 25.
5. Improved prompting did not consistently increase scores, particularly where the basic prompt was already clear and sufficient.

## 6. Overall Interpretation

The findings indicate that prompt design plays a useful role in shaping AI-generated responses. Improved prompts provide clearer expectations concerning audience, structure, content, length and output requirements.

Nevertheless, the results show that the effectiveness of improved prompting depends on the nature of the task. For straightforward mathematical problems, the basic prompt was sufficient to obtain accurate results. For creative writing and knowledge explanation, additional guidance had a more noticeable effect on some responses.

The comparison also indicates that neither platform achieved a perfect result in every experiment. Both produced responses that failed to meet at least one specified requirement, demonstrating the importance of reviewing AI-generated content rather than accepting it without evaluation.

Overall, the experiment supports the view that effective prompt design can improve the usefulness and control of AI-generated responses, although it does not guarantee complete compliance or consistently higher evaluation scores.
