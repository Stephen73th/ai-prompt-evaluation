# FINDINGS, RECOMMENDATIONS AND LIMITATIONS

## 1. Major Findings

The comparative evaluation of ChatGPT and Google Gemini produced the following findings:

### Finding 1: Improved prompts achieved a higher combined score

Improved prompts achieved 248 out of 250 (99.2%), compared with 245 out of 250 (98.0%) for basic prompts. This represents a three-point difference and suggests that clearer instructions and more specific requirements can improve response quality.

### Finding 2: ChatGPT achieved a marginally higher overall score

ChatGPT recorded 248 out of 250 (99.2%), while Gemini recorded 245 out of 250 (98.0%). Although ChatGPT achieved the higher score, the difference was relatively small and does not establish general superiority.

### Finding 3: The effect of prompt improvement varied across tasks

Improved prompts produced different outcomes depending on the task. Gemini improved in knowledge and explanation, while both platforms maintained their full scores in reasoning and complex instruction following. This indicates that prompt improvement does not produce identical results across different tasks.

### Finding 4: Basic prompts can be sufficient for clearly defined tasks

Both platforms achieved full marks in the reasoning and problem-solving task using the basic prompt. This demonstrates that a simple, clearly worded instruction can sometimes produce an accurate and complete response without extensive prompt engineering.

### Finding 5: AI responses still require human evaluation

Some responses failed to meet specific requirements, including word-count restrictions. This demonstrates that even detailed prompts cannot guarantee complete compliance. Human review remains important for identifying omissions, inaccuracies and unmet instructions.

## 2. Recommendations

Based on the experimental findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

### Recommendation 1: Use clear and specific prompts

Users should communicate their objectives clearly and include relevant details about the expected output. Specific instructions can reduce ambiguity and improve the usefulness of AI-generated responses.

### Recommendation 2: Include measurable requirements where necessary

When a task requires a particular word count, number of examples, format or structure, these requirements should be stated explicitly. Users should also verify that the generated response satisfies them.

### Recommendation 3: Match prompt complexity to task requirements

Not every task requires a lengthy prompt. Simple and clearly defined tasks may need only straightforward instructions, while more complex tasks may benefit from detailed constraints and structured guidance.

### Recommendation 4: Review AI-generated responses before use

Users should verify calculations, check factual claims, assess completeness and confirm compliance with instructions before relying on AI-generated content.

### Recommendation 5: Evaluate AI platforms across different tasks

Users and organisations should avoid selecting an AI platform based on a single response or task. Testing across different use cases provides a broader understanding of platform performance and limitations.

## 3. Limitations of the Study

Despite the useful findings, the experiment has several limitations.

### 3.1 Limited number of AI platforms

The evaluation considered only ChatGPT and Google Gemini. Other AI platforms were not included, so the results cannot represent the wider AI ecosystem.

### 3.2 Limited number of task categories

The experiment examined five task categories. Although these covered different capabilities, they do not represent every possible application of generative AI.

### 3.3 Small experimental sample

The study used 20 responses: five tasks, two prompt types and two AI platforms. This provides a useful comparison but is insufficient to establish broad or statistically generalisable conclusions.

### 3.4 Subjectivity in evaluation

Some evaluation criteria, particularly creativity, clarity and relevance, involve human judgement. Although a common scoring rubric was used, different evaluators might assign different scores to some responses.

### 3.5 Model and version differences

AI systems are regularly updated, and their responses may change across model versions. The findings therefore reflect the versions used during the experiment rather than permanent platform characteristics. The exact Gemini model version should be confirmed from the testing interface where possible.

### 3.6 Limited repetition of experiments

Each prompt condition was evaluated using one recorded response per platform and task. Repeated trials could produce different outputs and provide a more reliable basis for comparison.

## 4. Concluding Assessment

The experiment demonstrates that prompt design is an important factor in determining the quality, structure and relevance of AI-generated responses. Improved prompts achieved a slightly higher combined score than basic prompts, while both ChatGPT and Gemini performed strongly across the selected tasks.

However, the results also demonstrate that more detailed prompts do not automatically produce better outcomes in every situation. The effectiveness of a prompt depends on the task, the clarity of the original instruction and the ability of the AI system to follow the requirements.

The study therefore concludes that effective AI use requires a combination of clear prompt design, appropriate platform selection and human evaluation. AI-generated responses should be treated as outputs that require review rather than automatically accepted as complete or accurate.
