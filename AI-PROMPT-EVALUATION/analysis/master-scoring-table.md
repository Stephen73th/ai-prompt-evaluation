### 6. Preliminary Interpretation

The consolidated results show that both AI platforms performed strongly across the five experimental tasks. ChatGPT achieved 248 out of 250, while Gemini achieved 245 out of 250.

Improved prompts achieved a combined score of 248 out of 250, compared with 245 out of 250 for basic prompts. This represents a three-point difference across the ten improved-prompt experiments.

However, the results also show that improved prompts did not produce higher scores in every task. For example, both platforms achieved full marks in the reasoning and complex instruction-following tasks under both prompt conditions.

These findings suggest that prompt design can influence response quality, but its effect depends on the task and the quality of the initial prompt. The results should be interpreted cautiously because the experiment involved only five task categories and two AI platforms.
