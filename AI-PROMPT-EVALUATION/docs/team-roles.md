# Team Roles and Responsibilities

## Project Title
Prompt Matters: A Comparative Evaluation of AI Tools and the Impact of Prompt Design on Output Quality

## Team Structure

This project is undertaken by four squad members. Each member has a primary responsibility to ensure efficient execution, accountability, and collaboration throughout the experiment.

## Member 1 — Project Lead

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate project activities and team communication.
- Establish and monitor project milestones.
- Ensure all team members understand their assigned tasks.
- Monitor project progress against the mission requirements.
- Coordinate meetings and resolve workflow issues.
- Review the final project to ensure all acceptance criteria are addressed.
- Coordinate the final presentation or video.

### Primary Deliverable
Project coordination, progress tracking, and final project oversight.

## Member 2 — AI Tool Specialist

### Responsibilities
- Ensure access to the selected AI platforms.
- Confirm that both platforms are ready for testing.
- Run prompts consistently on ChatGPT and Google Gemini.
- Capture complete AI responses.
- Record experiment dates and relevant platform details.
- Organise the collected outputs in the repository.
- Report technical issues, unexpected behaviours, or inconsistencies encountered during testing.

### Primary Deliverable
Complete and organised experimental response records from both AI platforms.

## Member 3 — Prompt Engineer

### Responsibilities
- Design the five experimental task prompts.
- Develop basic and improved versions of each prompt.
- Ensure the prompts test different capabilities.
- Check prompts for clarity, fairness, and consistency.
- Ensure the same prompt wording is used across both platforms.
- Document the rationale for prompt design choices.
- Review prompt changes with the team before experiments begin.

### Primary Deliverable
A documented set of ten experimental prompts covering five task categories.

## Member 4 — Data Analyst

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain the response evaluation framework.
- Establish consistent scoring guidelines.
- Score AI responses using the agreed criteria.
- Verify mathematical or objectively testable outputs.
- Compare performance across tools and prompt conditions.
- Identify trends, strengths, weaknesses, and unexpected findings.
- Prepare tables, charts, and summaries for the final presentation.

### Primary Deliverable
Scoring records, comparative analysis, visualisations, and evidence-based findings.

## Shared Responsibilities

All team members are expected to:

- Participate in project discussions and reviews.
- Review experimental findings critically.
- Contribute to the final presentation or video.
- Document meaningful contributions in the shared repository.
- Communicate challenges and progress.
- Ensure conclusions are supported by actual experimental evidence.

## Collaboration Approach

The team will use the shared Gitea repository as its central workspace for project documentation, prompts, experiment records, analysis, and presentation materials.

Members should make meaningful commits that reflect their contributions. Project documents should be reviewed collaboratively to maintain consistency, quality, and transparency.

## Accountability

Each member is accountable for completing their assigned responsibilities and communicating progress to the Project Lead. Primary roles establish ownership but do not prevent other members from contributing to or reviewing tasks.
