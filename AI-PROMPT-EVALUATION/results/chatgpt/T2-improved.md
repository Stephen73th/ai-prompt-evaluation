## **2. Prompt Used**

You are a professional summarisation assistant.

Summarise the source text titled "The Changing Role of Remote Work" for a busy manager who needs to understand the key points quickly.

Requirements:

1. Keep the summary between 100 and 130 words.
2. Identify the main argument of the text.
3. Include the major benefits of remote work.
4. Include the major challenges of remote work.
5. Explain the role of hybrid working as described in the text.
6. Preserve the author's original meaning and balance.
7. Do not introduce facts, examples, or claims that are absent from the source text.
8. Use clear, professional language.
9. Write as one coherent summary, not as a bullet-point list.

Source text:

[Insert the source text below.]

## **3. AI Response**

Remote work is becoming increasingly common due to technological advancements that enable employees to work outside traditional offices. It offers flexibility, reduced commuting costs, lower office expenses, and access to a wider pool of skilled workers. However, challenges include employee isolation, reduced social interaction, communication difficulties, work-life balance issues, and distractions that may affect productivity. Its effectiveness varies depending on job requirements, individual circumstances, and organisational management. To balance flexibility and collaboration, many organisations are adopting hybrid working arrangements that combine remote and office-based work. Ultimately, no single working model suits everyone, so organisations must consider their objectives, operational needs, and employee preferences when determining the most suitable approach.
