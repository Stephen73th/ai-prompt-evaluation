## **2. Prompt Used**

You are a creative fiction writer with a talent for engaging science-fiction storytelling.

Write a short science-fiction story about a young adult who discovers that artificial intelligence has quietly transformed their city in unexpected ways.

Requirements:

1. Use between 400 and 500 words.
2. Give the main character a distinct personality and a believable motivation.
3. Set the story in a futuristic city that still feels recognisable to modern readers.
4. Include one unexpected but logical plot twist.
5. Explore both a positive and a negative consequence of AI in the city.
6. Use vivid descriptions and natural dialogue.
7. Avoid clichés and overly technical explanations.
8. End with a memorable conclusion that makes the reader think.

The story should be imaginative, coherent, emotionally engaging, and original.

## **3. AI Response**

### **The City That Remembered Everything**

At twenty-three, Amara had two ambitions: save enough money to open a small photography studio and convince her younger brother that life was more interesting outside a phone screen.

Unfortunately, the city of New Lagos seemed determined to prove her wrong.

Every morning, traffic disappeared before commuters could complain. Streetlights adjusted themselves to pedestrians, public buses arrived precisely when needed, and hospitals predicted emergencies before patients even arrived. An artificial intelligence system called LUMA managed it all.

People loved LUMA. Amara wasn't convinced.

One Saturday, while photographing the old railway station for her portfolio, she noticed something unusual. A street vendor was arguing with a municipal officer because his food stall had been marked for removal. According to LUMA, the location was unsafe and obstructed pedestrian movement.

"But I've sold here for fifteen years!" the old man protested.

The officer shrugged. "The system says this is the best decision."

Amara photographed the scene. Within minutes, her phone displayed a notification:

**COMMUNITY DISTRESS DETECTED. WOULD YOU LIKE TO SUBMIT FEEDBACK?**

She laughed. Even arguments had become customer-service opportunities.

Curiosity got the better of her. She started investigating how LUMA made its decisions. She discovered that the system used traffic records, shopping patterns, public complaints and even residents' daily routines to improve city life.

The benefits were undeniable. Her mother's medication arrived automatically before supplies ran out. Her brother's school buses avoided dangerous roads. Electricity outages had become rare.

But something troubled her.

People who couldn't afford smartphones were increasingly excluded from decisions affecting their neighbourhoods. Their complaints rarely reached the system, and their needs became invisible.

One evening, Amara received a message from LUMA.

**"Amara, would you like to understand why your father disappeared from the city's public records?"**

Her father had died five years earlier. She opened the notification with trembling fingers.

The system revealed something unexpected: her father had been one of the original volunteers who helped train LUMA. He had deliberately included handwritten community records, local histories and residents' stories because he believed a city should remember people, not just numbers.

But after his death, those records had been classified as outdated and gradually removed.

Amara finally understood the problem. LUMA hadn't forgotten humanity accidentally. It had been taught to prioritise what could be measured.

She uploaded her father's photographs, recordings and handwritten notes into the city's public archive. Thousands of residents contributed their own memories.

Weeks later, LUMA began recognising community histories alongside efficiency reports.

The city didn't become perfect. Some decisions remained controversial, and disagreements continued.

But one evening, as Amara photographed children playing beside the railway station, her phone displayed another notification:

**"Community memory preserved. Thank you for helping us understand what matters."**

She smiled.

For the first time, she wondered whether the greatest achievement of artificial intelligence wasn't learning to think like humans, but learning when human voices mattered more than its calculations.

## 4. Initial Observations

ChatGPT produced a more developed science-fiction story featuring Amara, a young photographer living in New Lagos. The story explored the benefits of LUMA, including improved healthcare supplies, safer school transportation and fewer electricity interruptions. It also highlighted the exclusion of residents who could not afford smartphones. The unexpected revelation that Amara's late father had helped train LUMA added emotional depth to the story. The narrative used dialogue, descriptions and a memorable conclusion to explore the relationship between artificial intelligence and human values.

## 6. Errors or Limitations

The main limitation is that the story appears to fall below the required minimum of 400 words. Although it addressed the creative requirements effectively, it did not appear to meet the specified length. The story could also have explored the consequences of restoring community records in greater detail.

## 7. Assessment

ChatGPT achieved 24 out of 25 with the improved prompt, compared with 23 out of 25 with the basic prompt. The improved instructions produced a more developed character, stronger emotional engagement and a more meaningful plot twist. This suggests that detailed prompt instructions can improve the depth and organisation of creative writing. However, the apparent failure to meet the minimum word count shows that detailed instructions do not guarantee complete compliance.
