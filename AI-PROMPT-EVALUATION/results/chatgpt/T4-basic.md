## 2. Prompt Used

A shop sells notebooks for ₦2,500 each and pens for ₦800 each. A customer buys 4 notebooks and 5 pens and receives a 10% discount on the total purchase. How much does the customer pay?

## 3. AI Response

### Step 1: Calculate the cost of notebooks

Price of one notebook = ₦2,500

Quantity = 4

4 × 2,500 = ₦10,000

### Step 2: Calculate the cost of pens

Price of one pen = ₦800

Quantity = 5

5 × 800 = ₦4,000

### Step 3: Calculate the total purchase cost

₦10,000 + ₦4,000 = ₦14,000

### Step 4: Calculate the 10% discount

10/100 × ₦14,000 = ₦1,400

### Step 5: Calculate the final amount

₦14,000 − ₦1,400 = ₦12,600

**Final Answer: ₦12,600**

The customer pays **₦12,600** after receiving the 10% discount.

## 4. Initial Observations

- ChatGPT correctly identified the individual costs of the notebooks and pens.
- It calculated the combined purchase cost before applying the discount.
- It correctly calculated the 10% discount.
- It presented the calculations in a logical sequence.
- It provided a clear final answer despite receiving only a basic prompt.
