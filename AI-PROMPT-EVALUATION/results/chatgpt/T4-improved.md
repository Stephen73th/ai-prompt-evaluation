## 2. Prompt Used

You are a careful mathematical problem-solving assistant. Solve the following shopping calculation accurately.

A shop sells:

- Notebooks at ₦2,500 each.
- Pens at ₦800 each.

A customer purchases 4 notebooks and 5 pens and receives a 10% discount on the entire purchase.

Instructions:

1. Calculate the total cost of the notebooks.
2. Calculate the total cost of the pens.
3. Calculate the combined cost before the discount.
4. Calculate the discount amount in naira.
5. Calculate the final amount payable.
6. Show each calculation clearly in the correct order.
7. Verify the final answer independently.
8. Do not make assumptions or skip any calculation.

Present the result in a clear, structured format and highlight the final amount the customer must pay.

## 3. AI Response

### Step 1: Calculate the Total Cost of Notebooks

- Price per notebook = ₦2,500
- Number of notebooks = 4

4 × ₦2,500 = ₦10,000

**Total cost of notebooks = ₦10,000**

### Step 2: Calculate the Total Cost of Pens

- Price per pen = ₦800
- Number of pens = 5

5 × ₦800 = ₦4,000

**Total cost of pens = ₦4,000**

### Step 3: Calculate the Combined Cost Before Discount

₦10,000 + ₦4,000 = ₦14,000

**Combined cost = ₦14,000**

### Step 4: Calculate the 10% Discount

10/100 × ₦14,000 = ₦1,400

**Discount amount = ₦1,400**

### Step 5: Calculate the Final Amount Payable

₦14,000 − ₦1,400 = ₦12,600

**Final amount payable = ₦12,600**

### Step 6: Independent Verification

A 10% discount means the customer pays 90% of the original amount.

90/100 × ₦14,000 = ₦12,600

The result matches the amount calculated in Step 5.

### Final Answer

**TOTAL AMOUNT THE CUSTOMER MUST PAY: ₦12,600**

After applying the 10% discount.

## 4. Initial Observations
