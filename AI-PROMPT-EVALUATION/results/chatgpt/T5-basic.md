## 2. Prompt Used

Create a one-day workshop schedule for beginners learning about artificial intelligence. Include the activities, session times, breaks, and lunch.

## 3. AI Response

# One-Day Artificial Intelligence Workshop Schedule

**Workshop Theme:** Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI) for Beginners

**Target Audience:** Beginners with little or no prior knowledge of AI.

**Duration:** 9:00 AM – 4:30 PM

**Goal:** Introduce participants to AI, explain how it works, explore everyday applications, and provide hands-on experience using AI tools.

### Workshop Schedule

| Time | Session / Activity | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 – 9:30 AM | Registration and Welcome | Participant registration, introductions, and workshop overview. |
| 9:30 – 10:00 AM | Introduction to Artificial Intelligence | Explain what AI is, its history, and why it matters. |
| 10:00 – 10:45 AM | Understanding How AI Works | Introduce machine learning, data, algorithms, and how AI systems learn. |
| 10:45 – 11:00 AM | Morning Break | Refreshments and networking. |
| 11:00 – 12:00 PM | Everyday Applications of AI | Explore AI in healthcare, education, banking, transportation, and social media. |
| 12:00 – 12:45 PM | Introduction to Generative AI Tools | Demonstrate tools such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini and explain what they can do. |
| 12:45 – 1:45 PM | Lunch Break | Lunch and relaxation. |
| 1:45 – 2:45 PM | Hands-on Practical Session | Participants practise writing prompts and using AI tools to answer questions, generate ideas, and summarise text. |
| 2:45 – 3:00 PM | Afternoon Break | Short refreshment break. |
| 3:00 – 3:45 PM | Responsible and Ethical Use of AI | Discuss misinformation, privacy, bias, plagiarism, and responsible AI use. |
| 3:45 – 4:15 PM | Group Activity and Presentation | Participants work in small groups to solve a simple problem using an AI tool and present their results. |
| 4:15 – 4:30 PM | Questions, Feedback and Closing | Recap key lessons, answer questions, collect feedback, and close the workshop. |

### Expected Learning Outcomes

By the end of the workshop, participants should be able to:

1. Explain what artificial intelligence means in simple terms.
2. Understand the basic principles behind how AI works.
3. Identify practical applications of AI in everyday life.
4. Use beginner-friendly AI tools such as ChatGPT and Gemini.
5. Write simple and effective prompts.
6. Recognise the limitations and risks of AI.
7. Apply AI responsibly in learning, work, and daily activities.

### Required Materials

- Laptop or smartphone for each participant.
- Stable internet connection.
- Projector and presentation slides.
- Access to AI tools such as ChatGPT or Google Gemini.
- Notebooks and writing materials.
- Power supply or charging facilities.

**Workshop Outcome:** Participants leave with a basic understanding of artificial intelligence and practical experience using AI tools to solve everyday problems.
