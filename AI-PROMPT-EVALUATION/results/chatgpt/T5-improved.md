## 2. Prompt Used

You are an experienced workshop planner. Create a detailed one-day introductory Artificial Intelligence (AI) workshop schedule for 20 beginners.

The workshop must run from 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM.

Follow these instructions carefully:

1. Include an opening session lasting exactly 15 minutes.
2. Include an introduction to artificial intelligence lasting exactly 60 minutes.
3. Include a prompt engineering session lasting exactly 60 minutes.
4. Include a practical AI activity lasting exactly 90 minutes.
5. Include a responsible AI session lasting exactly 45 minutes.
6. Include a final mini-project lasting exactly 30 minutes.
7. Include a closing session lasting exactly 15 minutes.
8. Include two separate 15-minute breaks.
9. Include one 45-minute lunch break.
10. Arrange all activities chronologically without overlapping sessions.
11. Ensure that the entire schedule fits exactly within the 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM timeframe.
12. Present the schedule in a table with these columns: Start Time, End Time, Duration, Activity.
13. After the table, provide exactly three learning outcomes for participants.
14. List exactly three materials participants will need.
15. Use simple, professional language suitable for beginners.

Important requirements:

- Do not omit any required activity or break.
- Do not change the specified session durations.
- Do not schedule two activities at the same time.
- Do not add activities that make the schedule exceed the available time.
- Check that all start and end times are consistent with the required durations.

Return only the completed workshop plan, including the schedule, learning outcomes, and required materials.

**Workshop Title:** Introduction to Artificial Intelligence for Beginners

| Start Time | End Time | Duration | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|

3. Notebook and writing materials.
