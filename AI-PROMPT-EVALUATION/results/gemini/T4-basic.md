## 2. Prompt Used

A shop sells notebooks for ₦2,500 each and pens for ₦800 each. A customer buys 4 notebooks and 5 pens and receives a 10% discount on the total purchase. How much does the customer pay?

## 3. AI Response

The customer pays ₦12,600.

Notebooks: 4 × ₦2,500 = ₦10,000

Pens: 5 × ₦800 = ₦4,000

Total before discount: ₦10,000 + ₦4,000 = ₦14,000

10% discount: 10% × ₦14,000 = ₦1,400

Final amount paid: ₦14,000 − ₦1,400 = ₦12,600
