## 2. Prompt Used

You are an experienced workshop planner. Create a detailed one-day introductory Artificial Intelligence (AI) workshop schedule for 20 beginners.

The workshop must run from 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM.

Follow these instructions carefully:

- Include an opening session lasting exactly 15 minutes.
- Include an introduction to artificial intelligence lasting exactly 60 minutes.
- Include a prompt engineering session lasting exactly 60 minutes.
- Include a practical AI activity lasting exactly 90 minutes.
- Include a responsible AI session lasting exactly 45 minutes.
- Include a final mini-project lasting exactly 30 minutes.
- Include a closing session lasting exactly 15 minutes.
- Include two separate 15-minute breaks.
- Include one 45-minute lunch break.
- Arrange all activities chronologically without overlapping sessions.
- Ensure that the entire schedule fits exactly within the 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM timeframe.
- Present the schedule in a table with these columns: Start Time, End Time, Duration, Activity.
- After the table, provide exactly three learning outcomes for participants.
- List exactly three materials participants will need.
- Use simple, professional language suitable for beginners.

Important requirements:

- Do not omit any required activity or break.
- Do not change the specified session durations.
- Do not schedule two activities at the same time.
- Do not add activities that make the schedule exceed the available time.
- Check that all start and end times are consistent with the required durations.

Return only the completed workshop plan, including the schedule, learning outcomes, and required materials.
